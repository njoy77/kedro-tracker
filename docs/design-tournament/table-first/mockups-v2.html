Kedro for iOS 26 · design tournament · round 2

Table First. Quiet at the table, loud after.

During play, Kedro is a scoreboard you glance at and a tally board you tap eight times. Afterwards, it's the record of who's been talking shit and whether they earned it. v2 keeps the v1 thesis and stock SwiftUI chrome, and replaces the identity and the voice: a members'-club racecard in bone, night, oxblood and brass; racing silks; a New York serif for things worth reading; and copy that roasts the play, never the person.

One dataset throughout. Noah's table (Velvet Rail): 5 players, with Sam as a guest, 6 rounds extended to 8. After Round 4 the totals are 36 / 20 / 38 / 34 / 43, and the final is Noah 28, Ana 34, Cleo 65, Ben 67, Sam 70. An 8-player Saturday table (Night Paddock) covers the large-table frames. Every number follows the canonical rules: a strictly lowest knocker scores −10 flat, a tied knocker scores the hand, and a beaten knocker scores hand + 10. All copy is from the deck in spec-v2.md §2.6, in the Unfiltered register unless a frame says Clean.
