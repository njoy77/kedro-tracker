Kedro · Table First

Design tournament entry for Kedro on iOS 26. During play, the app is a scoreboard you glance at and a keypad you use for fifteen seconds. Everything else waits until the cards are put away. It uses stock SwiftUI components, SF Pro and SF Symbols, Liquid Glass chrome, and brand red only on the one action that matters on each screen.

Sample game: Noah's Table, 5 players (Sam is a guest), 8 planned rounds. Every number follows the canonical rules. A knocker who is strictly lowest scores −10 flat, a tie scores the hand, and a beaten knocker scores hand + 10. Full rationale is in spec.md.
