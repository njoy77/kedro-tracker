Ambient Table

The game lives in the room, not on one screen. Phones near each other become one table, the knock gets recorded the moment it happens, and the score follows you to the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Twenty frames at 393×852 pt, iOS 26 with Liquid Glass. All the sample data comes from one real five‑player game (the full ledger is at the bottom of this page), and every score in it follows the canonical rules.
