Kedro · Horse Play

Mockups for the playful entry in the Kedro iOS design tournament (iOS 26, SwiftUI, Liquid Glass). During a round the app is fast and quiet. Between rounds it shows its personality: every player is a horse they made, standings run as a race to the lowest score, rewards are rosettes, and newcomers wait in the Paddock until their fifth game.

Full rationale, flows, and the SwiftUI implementation map are in spec.md. All scores below follow the canonical rules: J=11, Q=12, black K=13, red K=−1, Joker=0. A knocker scores −10 flat if strictly lowest, their hand if tied, and hand +10 if beaten.

  • Clean knock · −10
  • Tied knock · hand
  • Beaten knock · hand +10
  • Dashed ring = guest
