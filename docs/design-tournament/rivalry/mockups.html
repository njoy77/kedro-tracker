Kedro · iOS 26 design tournament · Entry: Rivalry Engine

Every game is a chapter in a rivalry.

20 frames of one evening at Noah's table: Noah, Maya, Jordan, Priya and Sam (a guest). Ten rounds, every score computed with the canonical rules (J 11 · Q 12 · black K 13 · red K −1 · Joker 0; a knocker scores −10 if strictly lowest, their hand on a tie, hand + 10 if beaten). Priya wins 33 to 34 with a final-round clean knock, in her 5th game, which puts her on the Track.
