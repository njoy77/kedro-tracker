Round 2 rebuilds the visual identity and voice from scratch. The horse comes through the racing form, owners’ silks and heraldry: an ink and bone palette, a single oxblood accent, gilt kept for honours, and New York serif headlines over SF Pro. The copy swears, roasts the play and never the person, and every line has a Clean twin. Same thesis as v1: stakes, then play, then payoff, then new stakes.

Sample data is one game in Scorekeeper mode (Sam is a guest with no phone): Noah (you), Maya, Jordan, Priya and Sam, 8 rounds extended to 10. Every number follows PRD 2.6 and was checked by script. Frames marked Clean show the cleaner-language option.

Rules of the look

  • Oxblood is the one accent: primary action, your position, the current round.
  • Knock outcomes and deltas are monochrome, told by sign, glyph, fill and word. Lower is better, so red/green would lie.
  • Gilt only for #1, winners and honours. Never on buttons.
  • Glass only on the controls layer. Scores sit on solid surfaces.
  • Hairlines, not shadows. Radii 10, 18, capsules for buttons. No blobs.
  • No mascots, no emoji, no confetti. The horse is a crest, not a character.
