Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Kedro is a score tracker for Cambio, the card game, played among friends: in the Kedro iPhone app and its App Clip, and on the web. This page explains what Kedro keeps about you and why.

What Kedro keeps

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Apple or Google, Kedro receives your name and email address. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, Kedro gets a private relay address instead.
  • Your profile. The name, handle and avatar (an emoji or a photo) you pick.
  • Your games. Who played, every round's scores, who dealt and who knocked, and when each game was played. Kedro uses these to work out results, stats, badges and the leaderboard.
  • Guest seats. When a host adds you as a guest, or you join from the App Clip without an account, Kedro keeps the first name used for your seat and your games, so you can claim them later with a claim code.
  • Safety records. Players you block, and anything you report.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications in the iPhone app, a device token so Kedro can send them.

On your phone

To find the table you're sitting at, the iPhone app talks to phones nearby over your local network, and, if you turn on nearby alerts, over Bluetooth. That only identifies the table and the players at it. Kedro doesn't use your location. The camera is used only when you scan a table's code or take a profile photo.

Who can see it

Other Kedro players can see your name, handle, avatar, the games you played with them, and your stats and place on the leaderboard. Other players never see your email address.

Who Kedro shares it with

Kedro doesn't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. It relies on a few services to run: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Apple and Google (sign-in), and Apple (iPhone notifications). They handle your information only to provide those services.

Google sign-in

Kedro asks Google only for your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to sign you in and set up your profile. Kedro's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your information

Kedro keeps your information while you have an account. To delete your account and profile, email noahjoy7@gmail.com from the address you sign in with.

Children

Kedro isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly keep information about them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email noahjoy7@gmail.com.
