Kedro Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Kedro is a score tracker for card games played among friends, on the web at kedro-tracker.vercel.app and in the Kedro iPhone app. This page explains what Kedro keeps about you and why.

What Kedro keeps

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Google or Apple, Kedro receives your name and email address. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, Kedro gets a private relay address instead.
  • Your profile. The name, handle and avatar (an emoji or a photo) you pick.
  • Your games. Who played, every round's scores, who dealt and who knocked, and when each game was played. Kedro uses these to work out results, stats and the leaderboard.
  • Safety records. Players you block, and anything you report.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications in the iPhone app, a device token so Kedro can send them.

Who can see it

Other Kedro players can see your name, handle, avatar, the games you played with them, and your stats and place on the leaderboard. Other players never see your email address.

Who Kedro shares it with

Kedro doesn't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. It relies on a few services to run: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Google and Apple (sign-in), and Apple (iPhone notifications). They handle your information only to provide those services.

Google sign-in

Kedro asks Google only for your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to sign you in and set up your profile. Kedro's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your information

Kedro keeps your information while you have an account. To delete your account and profile, email noahjoy7@gmail.com from the address you sign in with.

Children

Kedro isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly keep information about them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email noahjoy7@gmail.com.
